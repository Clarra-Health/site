A note from our founder
“We are living through the biggest wave of midlife transition in history, and it’s happening without a data infrastructure. Clarra is building it.”

Perimenopause is the most consequential health transition in a woman’s life, yet it remains chronically under-researched, under-funded and poorly supported. Women are navigating dozens of symptoms with fragmented care, dismissive providers and misinformation. The result: delayed diagnoses, worsening long-term health risks and millions of women managing midlife blindly and alone.

Not a symptom checker. Not another wellness app. The intelligence layer for midlife health.

Meghan White, Founder & CEO
